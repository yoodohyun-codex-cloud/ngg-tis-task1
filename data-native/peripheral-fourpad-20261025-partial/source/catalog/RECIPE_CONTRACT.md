# Proposed peripheral four-pad family

This source prepares five conditions for later finite selection. B0(square15,c10) and G1(square12,c10) are controls that must reuse verified first-eight results at the same drive amplitudes, ROI, material/contact and field ABI. P24 and P23.5 are equal225mm²-per-pad peripheral hypotheses; square12,c11.5 is a same144mm²-area spacing hypothesis against G1. Only three new geometries, at most six new driven RHS, are proposed. No local14/16mm proposal is reserved or assigned, and no new numerical worker is started.

The peripheral physical shape is the actual union of0.5mm square contact cells. Every corner must lie inside radius24mm or23.5mm and its positive-quadrant lowerx/y faces must be at least1mm from the axes. Version2 orders whole(x,y)↔(y,x) swap-orbits by descending exact centre radius-squared, then ascending|x-y|, then min(x,y). Equal-radius lattice orbits need not all be taken together. A fixed predeclared parity rule skips a two-cell orbit only when exactly one slot remains, records every such skip/radius, and takes the first later diagonal singleton. No orbit is split; no arbitrary lower-radius filler, post-result repair, area renormalization or widened radius is permitted. Exactly900 selected cells and final four-neighbour connectivity remain mandatory. If either cannot be achieved, the recipe is blocked. No masks or counts were evaluated before this policy was fixed.

The final physical union must be four-neighbour connected; corner-only bridges fail. Perimeter, internal hole components, actual centroid and bounds are reported. These are grid-defined polyomino contacts, not exact smooth annular sectors or validated manufacturable/safe shapes. The owner must actually derive count, area and connectivity proofs; the source author did not execute these calculations.

The positive pattern mirrors into four physical pads with fixed assignments: negative-negative output0, positive-positive output1, positive-negative and negative-positive common0V. There remain exactly two outputs with one50ohm resistor each, active output0 at2000Hz/output1 at2010Hz and the inactive output0V behind50ohm. The fixed50mm body/layers and existing frequency-specific material/contact receipts remain. All full contact faces have fraction1.

Equal-voltage[1,1] is the default to isolate geometry. If a future root decision selects unequal current allocation, equal-voltage control metrics cannot be silently reused: cached raw phasors may be rescaled and control metrics recalculated at that same allocation. No new PDE is needed for linear cached controls, but this is different from a full metric alias.

Periphery jointly changes shape, centroid, gap, coverage and perimeter. Matched area is not proof of an isolated causal effect. Preserve fixed ROI, absolute target mean/P10, non-target distributions, burden-normalized absolute modulation and prior numerical/material/contact/edge-Jmax uncertainties. A blocked recipe stays listed with its exact reason. The model cannot proceed automatically from source readiness to actual execution, candidate adoption or phantom enlargement.
